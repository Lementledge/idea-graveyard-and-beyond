# Modular Recursive Extensibility: A Non-Mutating Core Framework for AGI

> **One-sentence Abstract:** Artificial General Intelligence does not require internal weight mutation or monolithic architectural rewiring; instead, it can emerge from a stable, non-mutating core equipped with representational extensibility, grounded verifiability, and recursive modular tool synthesis.

- **Status:** `Formulated Hypothesis`
- **Domain:** Algorithm / Cognitive Systems / AGI Theory
- **Primary Bottleneck:** Formalization of Dynamic Domain-Specific Languages (DSLs) & Automated Grounded Verifiers
- **Created Date:** 2026-10-12

---

## 1. Context & Motivation

Current paradigms in Artificial General Intelligence research frequently oscillate between two extremes:
1. **Monolithic Scaling:** Scaling dense foundation models within fixed token vocabularies and invariant latent dimensionalities ($$d_{\text{model}}$$), resulting in combinatorial recombination without true conceptual novelty.
2. **Unconstrained Self-Modification:** Theoretical recursive self-improvement models that attempt to rewrite their own internal weights or core computational graphs, which inevitably face catastrophic forgetting, optimization collapse, and alignment unpredictability.

Human biological intelligence demonstrates that general intelligence does not require rewiring foundational cortical structures when acquiring higher-order capabilities. Biological humans do not modify neural substrates directly to formulate calculus; rather, they externalize representations, construct symbolic tools, and verify them against physical reality. General intelligence should therefore be framed as an **externalizing modular synthesis engine** rather than a self-modifying monolithic system.

---

## 2. Core Hypothesis & Mechanics

### Foundational Triad
A general intelligence architecture requires at least three non-negotiable operational conditions:

1. **Representational Extensibility:**
   - The capacity to invent novel representation systems, grammars, data structures, and symbolic formalisms when existing token spaces and latent priors are insufficient.
   - The system must not remain bounded by its initial vocabulary, latent coordinate frame, or static ontological primitives.

2. **Grounded Verifiability:**
   - Representations and claims must possess verifiable interfaces with reality, formal systems, or empirical feedback loops.
   - Prevents unconstrained semantic drift and plausible confabulation by enforcing formal falsifiability (e.g., interaction with compilers, physical sensors, formal proof assistants, or mathematical invariants).

3. **Recursive Toolmaking (Bootstrapping):**
   - The capability to leverage current-tier computational competence (N) to construct, test, and register specialized modules that operate at competence (N+1).
   - Intelligence scales through the accumulation of externalized, composable functional units rather than unbounded internal parameter expansion.

### Architectural Invariant: Stable Core, Dynamic Ecosystem
- **The Core Engine:** Acts as an invariant reasoning substrate, compiler, and orchestration runtime. It maintains strict invariants and does not mutate its underlying neural parameters.
- **The Peripheral Modular Web:** A dynamically expanding registry of domain-specific languages (DSLs), deterministic routines, isolated micro-agents, and verified symbolic schemas synthesized directly by the core.

---

## 3. Potential Impact & Value

- **Auditability and Modular Safety:** Dangerous, erroneous, or drifting behaviors are confined to isolated peripheral modules that can be formally audited, sandbox-tested, or excised without destabilizing the core runtime.
- **Escape from Latent Bottlenecks:** Circumvents the semantic saturation of fixed-dimensional vector spaces by allowing the model to generate bespoke external formalisms tailored to novel problem domains.
- **Energy and Compute Efficiency:** Complex subroutines are compiled into deterministic, reusable algorithmic artifacts rather than re-inferred probabilistically via autoregressive forward passes.

---

## 4. The Bottlenecks (Why It's Beyond Current Reach)

- **Theoretical Limits:**
  - Lack of a formal criterion to determine *when* an existing representation space is saturated, necessitating the synthesis of a novel representation system.
  - Absence of universal translation protocols between heterogeneous, dynamically generated symbolic modules.
- **Empirical & Engineering Constraints:**
  - Building open-ended verifiable environments that cover abstract, non-code domains (e.g., sociodynamics, open-world physical dynamics) where binary ground-truth verifiers do not exist.
  - High engineering friction in synthesizing robust runtime sandboxes on the fly without unbounded latency overhead.

---

## 5. Potential Attack Vectors & Next Steps

- **Minimal Toy Model:**
  - Build a frozen language model runtime paired with a restricted programming environment (e.g., Python + Lean 4 / Z3 SMT solver).
  - Challenge the system to solve an escalating sequence of non-standard discrete graph puzzles by iteratively synthesizing, verifying, and storing new procedural tools in a persistent library (similar to an autonomous Voyager/Voynich architecture, but expanded to dynamic DSL generation).
- **Open Questions:**
  1. What minimal formal structure must the "Stable Core" possess to guarantee it can generate modules strictly more expressive than its initial training set?
  2. How can the system prevent infinite regression in recursive tool validation (avoiding the problem of verifying the verifier)?
